01. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how ANONYMOUS CARD handles information for accounts, game features, purchases and support. The information involved depends on your platform and the features you use.

Google Play Billing, rewarded ads (Quick Jobs) and consent management for Android are currently in development. The relevant sections describe how information is handled if those features are made available. Describing a development-stage feature does not mean it is currently available to the public.

03. Accounts and authentication

Depending on the account method, we handle usernames, account identifiers, email addresses, password hashes, SteamIDs and information needed for authentication and sessions. These support account management, login, identity verification and abuse prevention.

  • Steam-only registration uses a SteamID and username without requiring an email address or password.
  • When email is used, it supports registration verification, account-change notices and password recovery. Registered email addresses are not used to send advertising email.
  • Adding email/password login stores a pending email, password hash and verification-code hash. The code is valid for 15 minutes; expiry does not mean that the stored data is deleted at that time.
  • New Steam registration records the versions of the Terms and Privacy Policy acknowledged, and the acknowledgement time.

Passwords are hashed. Authentication tokens and recovery information support login, session continuity and account recovery.

04. Game data, UGC and visibility

We handle owned cards, Coins and tickets, decks, progress, match times and outcomes, rank/rating, friends and requests, avatar settings and other data needed to save, synchronize and provide the game and matches.

User-generated content (UGC), such as created cards, may include titles, descriptions or rules, images/pixel art, visibility settings and a creator display name. Public card listings also involve public identifiers and reaction counts. Likes and notifications involve user/card associations, timestamps and read status.

Usernames are displayed in features such as matches. The visibility of cards and creator display names depends on the feature and settings. Other users can view published content. Do not include personal information in card titles, descriptions, images or display names.

Some information is stored on your device, including drafts, presets and records used to retry or recover operations. Removing local information or uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete the server account or records.

05. Purchases and balances

Windows purchases use Steam. The game handles products, amounts, times, purchase status, transaction/order identifiers, account associations and grant results for purchase verification, balances, duplicate-grant prevention and support.

Google Play Billing for Android is in development. If made available, the implementation uses product information, purchase tokens, order information, purchase status and account associations to verify purchases with Google on the server, and records grant results and transactions. Incomplete purchases can be retried or recovered.

The game is not designed to collect payment-method credentials such as credit-card numbers. Information handled directly by payment providers is subject to their own policies.

06. Android ads and consent

IN DEVELOPMENT · NOT IN PRODUCTION

Quick Jobs is being developed as an optional rewarded-ad feature for Android. The implementation uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and UMP to check consent information.

Depending on settings and conditions of use, the advertising SDK may send to and process with Google information such as IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), device/account identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information. This supports advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no data is processed.

Available consent messages and choices depend on factors such as region, age-related settings and applicable requirements. The absence of a consent form is not treated as an affirmative consent decision.

Reward verification uses information including a reward-session identifier associated with the account, ad unit, viewing/reward information and transaction identifier. Server-verified results and receipt records support duplicate prevention and usage limits. A client-side completion notification alone does not establish a reward.

  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

When advertising becomes available, consent and privacy settings will be explained according to region, age and other conditions of use. Review your settings using the available choices.

07. Logs, maintenance and support

Technical records can include request identifiers, processed features/routes, response status, processing time and transfer size for maintenance, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Account activity and purchase account/product/order/status records may also be handled.

The implementation logs email recipients and delivery results. Support requests involve contact information, messages and other relevant information you provide.

The API processes IP addresses for rate limiting and abuse prevention. Hosting and network infrastructure logs may also include IP addresses. Game-generated records are distinct from records handled by hosting or other infrastructure providers.

08. External services and sharing

Services used as needed to provide the game include Steam (authentication/payments), hosting/network infrastructure and email delivery. If Android purchases and ads are made available, processing will also involve Google purchase verification, advertising and consent management.

The game’s server environment uses Xserver VPS. Information handled by external services, storage locations and any processing abroad depend on the services, contracts and settings. Information is handled in accordance with applicable law, including required oversight of service providers and disclosures.

Personal data is not provided to third parties without consent unless permitted by law. Information is handled within necessary purposes, including legally distinct arrangements such as processing entrusted to service providers.

If you contact support through Discord or X, information you send is also handled by that service. Do not send confidential information in public posts.

09. Retention and security

Account and game data are retained for as long as needed to provide, synchronize and recover the service. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or, where appropriate, anonymized.

When handling deletion requests, information may be retained only to the minimum extent and for the minimum period necessary for legal obligations, abuse prevention, prevention of duplicate purchase/reward processing or transaction integrity. Where information must be retained, the information, reason and retention period or criteria will be explained individually.

Logs and backups are also handled according to maintenance, recovery and security needs. Where they require separate handling from ordinary deletion, remaining information and its treatment are assessed and explained where necessary.

Security measures include password hashing, appropriate access controls and protection of communications and stored information. No guarantee that all risks are eliminated is made.

10. Deletion and other requests

For account/data deletion, access or correction requests, contact anonymouscard18@gmail.com. See the deletion-request guidance for instructions.

The operator handles deletion individually and manually after verifying account ownership. There is currently no in-game account-deletion feature. An email request does not require an X account or reinstalling the app.

As a general rule, identity verification and deletion handling are completed within 30 days of receiving the request email. If additional checks or other circumstances require more time, we will explain the reason and any additional verification needed.

Information retained for legal obligations, abuse prevention or transaction integrity is limited to the minimum necessary, with its reasons and handling explained. If a request cannot be fulfilled, the reason will also be explained.

11. Minors and policy updates

Minors should review this policy with a parent or guardian. Guardian involvement or age-appropriate handling may be required depending on the features used and applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy will be communicated through updates to this page and appropriate in-game notices where needed. Material changes will be appropriately announced.
