Account & data deletion

How to request deletion of your ANONYMOUS CARD account and data.

Effective upon publication of this page

01. Operator and app

App: ANONYMOUS CARD
Operator: ルーたん（Rutan）
Deletion and privacy contact: anonymouscard18@gmail.com
General inquiries: Rutan_youtube on X

02. How to request deletion

Send requests to: anonymouscard18@gmail.com

Use the subject “ANONYMOUS CARD Account Deletion” and provide your in-game username, login method (such as Steam or email), and that you wish to request account/data deletion.

There is currently no in-game account-deletion feature. The operator handles each request manually after verifying account ownership. Email requests do not require an X account or reinstalling the app. X direct messages are not the sole deletion-request channel.

03. Identity verification

We verify that the request comes from the account owner to prevent deletion by another person. If additional information is needed to identify the account or verify ownership, we will ask individually for the minimum necessary information.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, purchase tokens, credit-card numbers or unnecessary identity documents. Do not post account information publicly on X.

04. Assessing deletion scope

After verifying ownership, we assess related data according to the request. The list below describes categories considered when determining deletion, anonymization or necessary retention. The specific scope is determined and explained individually.

  • Account information: username, email, authentication/linking information and profile.
  • Game information: progress, owned cards/balances and decks.
  • Created/shared information: UGC cards, creator display names, images, likes and notifications.
  • Related records: matches/friends, activity and support records.

For records involving other users, such as match and shared records, effects on those users’ data are considered separately. We assess and explain the scope of deletion, anonymization or necessary retention. Necessary transaction and similar records may be retained as described below.

Device-local drafts and presets may be stored separately from server information. Any necessary device-side steps will be explained individually. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete the server account.

05. Minimum necessary retention

Purchase, reward and processing-result information may be retained only to the minimum extent and for the minimum period necessary for legal obligations, abuse prevention, prevention of duplicate processing or transaction integrity.

We will explain the retained information, reasons and period or criteria individually. If logs, backups or similar records need separate treatment from ordinary deletion, remaining information and necessary handling are assessed. We do not have a policy of retaining unnecessary information indefinitely.

06. Timing and completion

As a general rule, identity verification and deletion handling are completed within 30 days of receiving the request email.

If additional checks or other circumstances require more time, we will explain the reason and any additional verification needed. Requests are handled individually after verification; sending an email does not instantly remove the account or all data.

We will send the outcome to your reply address. If any information cannot be deleted, we will explain the reason and the extent of retention.
